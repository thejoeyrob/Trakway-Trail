Quick answers

How do I steer?Swipe left or right. One swipe moves you one lane position. Double-tap or press HONK to sound the horn.
The sheep (or cows) aren’t moving when I honk.Wait for the baa (or moo), then honk. You can honk from just before it until the herd is almost on you. A better horn from the garage widens the early window.
What does Drop Trailer do?It makes your cab faster and quicker to steer, but any unbanked delivery is lost. A new job (or a green hitch) hooks the trailer back on.
Is my progress saved?Yes, on your device. If you delete the app, the progress goes with it.
Does the game cost anything?No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.
Sound is missing.Check the silent switch is off and the volume is up, then restart the app. Sound effects and music have separate sliders in In the cab (settings).
