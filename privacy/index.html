Truckin’ & Drivin’ — Privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026 · from JW EDS

The short version

The iPhone and Android apps collect nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (garage wallet, unlocked trucks, paint, upgrades, settings, best shifts and the driver name you type in) is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

iPhone and iPad

Game Center is optional and run by Apple. If you sign in and submit a score, Apple’s Game Center privacy terms apply. Everything else works offline.

Android

The app asks for no permissions except vibration (for haptic feedback). It does not use the internet.

The web version

If you play in a browser, the page may send your chosen driver name and score to the online leaderboard along with a random player ID. Don’t use your real name if you don’t want it shown. No other data is collected.

Children

The game contains no ads, links, chat or purchases. It is not directed at a particular age group and contains only cartoon slapstick.
