Truckin’ & Drivin’ puts you in the cab of a very big lorry on some very small roads. Take delivery jobs, follow the golden trail, bank your payday, and try not to meet anything with horns, wool or a 99 Flake.

SWIPE. DODGE. HONK.
Five lane positions, one swipe at a time. Read the road, pick your route at the forks, and keep that trailer under control.

A ROAD THAT KEEPS CHANGING
Bends, forks, roadworks, villages and river bends across towns, countryside, estates and construction sites. No two shifts are the same.

THE FUNNIEST HAZARDS ON THE ROAD
• Honk in time to scatter sheep, cows, geese, Morris dancers and cyclists.
• Dodge low bridges, weak bridges, bus lanes, one-way streets, tractors, caravans, hay bales and bin lorries.
• Every shift-ending crash is a cartoon: “OH, FLOCK!”, “MOO-VE OVER!”, “KNOCK, KNOCK!” and “99 PROBLEMS!”

EARN, UPGRADE, SHOW OFF
• Deliveries, double trailers, night shifts, turbo teas and taco breaks.
• Drop the trailer to run light and fast, then hitch up again for the next job.
• A garage that shows your rig exactly as it drives: separate cab and trailer paint, roof graphics and nine upgrades you can actually see.
• Preview anything, even before you can afford it.

KEY FEATURES
• Arcade trucking with real-world UK road challenges
• Dynamic routes with split-path decisions
• Funny shift-ending hazards and comic crash scenes
• Garage and customisation
• Daily dispatch and leaderboard
• Tall-phone view and iPad support
• No ads. No in-app purchases. Works offline.
